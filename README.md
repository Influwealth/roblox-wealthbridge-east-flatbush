# 🏙️ Roblox WealthBridge: East Flatbush Capsule World

> **A Roblox experience where you learn finance, building, and community planning while earning real Community Credits!**

---

## 🎮 What Is This?

This is a **Roblox game** set in a 90s-style East Flatbush neighborhood where you:
- ✅ Complete **quests** to learn about money, technology, and community building
- ✅ Earn **Community Credits** (like digital punch cards!)
- ✅ Redeem credits for **real-world benefits** (with guardian approval)
- ✅ Build your own districts and share them with friends

## 📦 What's Inside?

### 🎮 **Roblox Game** (`roblox/`)
- 6 Districts: Finance Lab, Maker Yard, Telecom Tower, Civic Commons, Marketplace, Events Hub
- Quest system with rewards
- Community Credits economy
- Multiplayer co-op missions

### 🌐 **Gateway API** (`gateway/`)
- Connects Roblox to WealthBridge OS
- Handles credit redemptions safely
- Tracks all quests and activities
- Guardian consent system

### 🖥️ **MCP Dashboard** (`mcp-ui/`)
- Visual tracker for your quests
- See your Community Credits balance
- Guardian approval panel
- Activity logs

## 🎯 Districts (Where You Play)

1. **💰 Finance Lab** — Learn budgeting at a 90s check-cashing spot
2. **🔨 Maker Yard** — Build solar lamps and DIY projects
3. **📡 Telecom Tower** — Set up mesh networks on rooftops
4. **🏛️ Civic Commons** — Plan community projects at the gym
5. **🛒 Marketplace** — Run a corner store with fair pricing
6. **🎉 Events Hub** — Host block parties and co-op missions

---

## 🛡️ Safety & Compliance

- ✅ **No pay-to-win** — Earn credits through quests only
- ✅ **Guardian consent required** for redemptions
- ✅ **All activities are logged** for safety
- ✅ **No personal info required** to play
- ✅ **Moderated chat only**
